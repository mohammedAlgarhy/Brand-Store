## ✨ **Overview**

**Golden Hour** is a modern, high-performance e-commerce platform for a premium fashion brand.  
It delivers a luxurious shopping experience with elegant design, smooth animations, full bilingual support (English/Arabic), and a robust cart system.

Built with **Next.js 16**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**, the store is fully optimized for speed, SEO, and responsiveness across all devices.

---

## 🚀 **Key Features**

- 🛍️ **Product Catalog** – Browse collections (Spring, Summer, All Products) with powerful filtering and sorting.
- 🛒 **Shopping Cart** – Add/remove items, update quantities, and see total price in real-time.
- 🌍 **Bilingual Support** – Seamless toggle between English and Arabic (RTL layout for Arabic).
- ❤️ **Wishlist** – Save favorite items to a persistent wishlist using `localStorage`.
- 🎨 **Custom Animations** – Smooth scroll-triggered animations with GSAP.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive** – Optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- 🔍 **Advanced Filtering** – Filter by category, price range, and sale items.
- 🖼️ **Dynamic Product Modal** – View product details without leaving the page.
- 💳 **Demo Checkout** – Integrated payment form simulation.
- 📄 **Dynamic Routing** – Dedicated pages for each product, collection, and info.
